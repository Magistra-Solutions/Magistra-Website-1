Cookie Information

1. How this website uses storage

This website does not intentionally deploy advertising, profiling or audience-measurement cookies. It does not embed Google Analytics or marketing pixels.

2. Language preference

If you select a language, the preference is saved in your browser’s localStorage under magistraLang so subsequent pages can use your selected language. This is functional storage for your requested language preference, not tracking. You may clear it using your browser’s site-data controls.

3. Essential technical operation

The hosting platform may process necessary technical connection and security data to serve the pages. These operations are distinct from optional advertising or analytics tracking.

4. External services and consent

WhatsApp and Google Calendar are linked externally; neither is embedded as a tracker on these pages. If the site later adds non-essential cookies or comparable tracking, consent options and this information will be updated before activation. Since the current website does not use such technologies, there is no cookie-consent banner.
