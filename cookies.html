Cookie Policy

Important: This website package intentionally includes no analytics, advertising pixels or third-party tracking scripts. A final production deployment should still be technically checked before this statement is published unchanged.

1. Current V1 configuration

The supplied V1 code does not intentionally set analytics, advertising or marketing cookies and does not include Google Analytics, LinkedIn Insight Tag or a consent management platform.

2. Strictly necessary technologies

Technical infrastructure used by the production hosting environment may still involve technologies necessary to deliver or secure the website. Those should be inventoried once the final hosting and deployment stack is known.

3. No decorative consent banner

A cookie banner is not included merely for appearance. If non-essential cookies or trackers that require consent are later introduced, Magistra should implement an appropriate consent mechanism and update this policy before activation.

4. Future categories

The legal architecture can later be extended to distinguish necessary, analytics, marketing and third-party technologies. Categories should be described as active only when they are actually implemented.

5. Change management

Before activating analytics, tracking pixels, embedded video, maps, CRM forms, newsletter tools or similar services, Magistra should review cookies, consent, privacy, providers, transfers, security and retention.
